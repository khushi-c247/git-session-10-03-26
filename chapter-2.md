By afternoon, Ai was ready to push the branch and open a pull request.

Ai pulled the updated main branch and rebased the work to maintain a clean history. During the rebase, a merge conflict appeared in a shared utility file.

Instead of rushing, Ai reviewed both changes carefully:

One update improved performance.

The teammate’s change improved error handling.

Ai combined both improvements, tested locally, and completed the rebase successfully.
